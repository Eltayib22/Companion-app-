Optional: your own adhan sound
==============================

Put a short adhan recording here before running tool/setup_native.dart:

  Android:  adhan/adhan.mp3   (the setup script copies it into the Android app)
  iPhone:   adhan/adhan.caf   (add it to the Runner target in Xcode; see README.md)

Notification sounds have limits: iPhone plays at most 30 seconds, and
Android plays the file once. A short clip of the opening takbir works best.
Use a recording you have permission to use.

Then turn on Settings > Alerts > "Use my adhan recording" in the app.
